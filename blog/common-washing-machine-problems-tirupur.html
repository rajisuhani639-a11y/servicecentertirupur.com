Common Washing Machine Problems in 2026: Causes & Quick Fixes | Tirupur Guide

From heavy workwear wash loads in Kumar Nagar to borewell hard water scaling in Nallur, washing machines in Tirupur face daily stress. Here is an easy 2026 troubleshooting guide to common faults and when you need technician help.

Why Is Your Washing Machine Not Draining Water?

In most Tirupur homes across Avinashi Road and 15 Velampalayam, a washing machine stopping with water trapped inside the drum is usually caused by small coins, safety pins, or fabric lint jammed inside the bottom drain pump filter.

When the pump impeller cannot rotate freely, the control board pauses the cycle and beeps with a drain error. Before booking service, place a dry towel on the floor, open the bottom access flap, unscrew the round filter plug slowly, and clean out any trapped debris. If water still refuses to drain, the drain pump motor coil may have burnt out and needs on-site replacement.

Vibrating Violently and Jumping During Spin Cycle

If your front load or top load washer shakes uncontrollably and makes loud banging noises during high-speed spinning, the internal shock absorbers or tub suspension springs have lost tension.

In older machines, a cracked cast-aluminum drum spider or worn rear bearings allow the stainless steel drum to wobble off-center. Technicians inspect the suspension dampers and level the adjustable rubber feet to prevent machine walking and cabinet denting.

Water Entering Very Slowly or Error E01 Appearing

Tirupur's groundwater contains dissolved minerals that form white crusty scale inside the water inlet valve filter mesh. Even when your overhead tank is full, water trickles slowly into the detergent drawer.

Disconnecting the inlet hose from the back of the washer and gently brushing the small plastic mesh filter with an old toothbrush under running water often restores strong water flow immediately.

Things You Can Safely Check at Home

1

Clean the Bottom Drain Filter

Unscrew the lower round plug, let trapped water drain into a tray, and remove stuck coins or lint.

2

Check Water Inlet Filter Mesh

Turn off the tap, disconnect the inlet hose, and clean mineral scale from the fine mesh screen.

3

Verify Machine Leveling

Ensure all four rubber feet sit flat on the floor without wobbling when pushed diagonally.

4

Avoid Overloading Clothes

Leave at least one hand-width space between clothes and the top of the drum for free movement.
