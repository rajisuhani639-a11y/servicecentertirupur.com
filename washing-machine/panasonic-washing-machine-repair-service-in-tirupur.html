Common Panasonic Washing Machine Problems We Check in Tirupur

If your Panasonic washing machine shows any of these common symptoms, our technicians inspect the components to identify the exact cause:

Panasonic U11 Drain Error

Machine cannot drain water within the timeout period and beeps U11. Caused by coins, lint, or safety pins choking the drain pump or valve flap.

Panasonic U12 Lid Open / Lock Error

Machine will not spin or start cycle because the lid safety sensor cannot verify the door is closed. The magnetic lid switch needs check.

Panasonic U13 Unbalanced Spin Error

The machine halts before spinning and shakes heavily. Caused by tangled laundry or worn-out suspension damper rods.

Panasonic U14 Water Supply Error

Water is not entering the drum in time. Caused by low tap pressure, choked mesh filter, or a burnt inlet solenoid coil.

Panasonic Drum Not Turning / Motor Silent

The timer advances but the drum or pulsator will not move. Caused by a snapped drive belt, worn motor capacitor, or inverter board issue.

Panasonic Water Leaking From Bottom

Water drips continuously under the machine onto the floor. Caused by a cracked drain pipe, loose hose clamp, or torn spin shaft buffer seal.

Panasonic Technical Troubleshooting & Fault Guidance in Tirupur North Railway Feeder

Water drainage failure is usually caused by small coins, hairpins, or lint choking the bottom drain pump filter, or a jammed drain motor impeller. Cleaning the filter on-site restores normal drainage.

A loud rumbling noise during high-speed spinning indicates worn-out tub bearings, a cracked drum spider, or damaged suspension shock absorbers. Replacing bearings and dampers eliminates the noise.

Wait 3 minutes after switching off the power for the PTC thermal lock to disengage. If it remains jammed, our technician can perform a safe manual release and replace the faulty door interlock switch.

Violent shaking during spin is caused by unevenly distributed laundry, worn-out hydraulic suspension dampers, weak tub balance springs, or broken drum spider arms. Ensuring level feet and replacing weak suspension rods restores stability.
