IFB Home Appliance Service & Common Faults in Tirupur

If the power light is ON but the machine won't start the cycle, the most common culprit is the door interlock safety switch failing to confirm latching, a stuck child lock, or low water pressure triggering the inlet safety timeout.

Drainage failure is primarily caused by coins, safety pins, or heavy fabric lint trapped inside the bottom drain pump filter. In some cases, hard water sediment jams the pump impeller or the drain motor winding has burnt out.

If the machine washes but refuses to spin, the control board has likely detected an unbalanced load, the drain pump has not fully emptied water within the timeout period, or the drive belt / motor carbon brushes are worn out.

A loud airplane or helicopter rumbling noise during high-speed spinning indicates failed drum bearings and a compromised water seal. If left unaddressed, leaking water can corrode the drum spider and short-circuit the drive motor.

IFB machines use a thermal bi-metal door lock that remains locked for 2 to 3 minutes after cycle completion. If it remains stuck, water may still be present in the tub, or the PTC lock switch has fused. Our technicians can manually release the door without breaking the handle.

Common IFB codes: dE / dLEr indicates a door lock or latch issue; E01 / Er01 indicates no water filling within 5 minutes; E02 indicates drainage timeout; E03 indicates water temperature or heater element fault; and E04 indicates motor rotation or tachometer sensor mismatch.
