Common Sansui TV Problems Repaired in Tirupur

Frequent issues faced by Sansui television owners in Tirupur and how our local technician inspects them.

Display Defect Fault #1

Sansui Screen Shows Bright Circular Light Spots

What Happens: Several bright white spots like circular coins shine through the picture, noticeable during bright scenes or cricket matches.

Why It Happens: Acrylic optical diffuser lenses mounted over individual LED backlight diodes have detached due to heat and fallen to the bottom of the panel.

On-Site inspection: Technician opens the optical diffuser layers, cleans old adhesive, refits optical lenses with UV heat-resistant cement, or replaces the strip.
Power Circuit Fault #2

Sansui TV Completely Dead with No Standby Glow

What Happens: Power cord is plugged in, but the red indicator light does not glow at all and TV does not respond to remote or cabinet buttons.

Why It Happens: Swollen electrolytic capacitors, blown mains fuse, or shorted rectifier diode on the Sansui SMPS power board.

On-Site inspection: Technician tests AC line fuse, 300V primary filter capacitor, and 12V standby power rail, replacing damaged parts on-site.
Backlight Burnout Fault #3

Left Half of Sansui TV Screen Dim or Dark

What Happens: The right side of the screen shows normal picture, but the left side is noticeably darker with shadowy horizontal bands.

Why It Happens: One branch of the parallel LED backlight circuit has burnt out, dropping illumination on one side of the panel.

On-Site inspection: Measures current draw across left and right LED channels and installs a balanced replacement backlight set.
App Crash Fault #4

Sansui Smart TV Restarts When Opening Hotstar / YouTube

What Happens: Regular cable TV channels work fine, but opening any streaming app triggers an immediate crash and TV restart.

Why It Happens: Video decoding RAM chip instability or voltage drop on the motherboard 1.8V DDR power rail under heavy load.

On-Site inspection: Tests DDR voltage stability under stress test, replaces filtering capacitors near RAM, and clears app runtime cache.
Audio Issue Fault #5

Tinny Raspy Sound from Sansui Internal Speakers

What Happens: Sound is thin, lacks low frequencies, and distorts with harsh metallic vibration at volumes above 25 percent.

Why It Happens: Speaker voice coil has overheated and deformed, causing friction against the magnetic pole piece.

On-Site inspection: Tests speaker voice coil DC resistance and fits a replacement stereo speaker pair matched to Sansui audio amplifier.
Signal Port Fault #6

Sansui TV AV / HDMI Input Shows 'No Signal'

What Happens: Cable set-top box is powered on, but television shows blue screen with 'No Signal' banner across AV or HDMI.

Why It Happens: Physical pin detachment on connector socket or blown ESD diode on input line following lightning storm.

On-Site inspection: Inspects port solder joints under magnification, resolders connection tracks, and tests video decoder input.
