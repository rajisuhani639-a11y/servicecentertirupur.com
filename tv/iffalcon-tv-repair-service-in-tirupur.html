Backlight Failure Fault #1

Dialogue Audible but iFFALCON Screen Stays Pitch Black

Customer Complaint: Serial dialogue and news broadcasts are loud and clear, but the panel displays zero light. Shining a flashlight reveals faint outlines.

Defect Analysis: Series-connected LED diodes inside the backlight strips have burned open, cutting current and forcing the driver into shutdown.

Technician fix: Analyzes individual LED diode drops using an electronic backlight tester and fits a replacement set of copper-backed lighting bars.
Smart OS Fault #2

iFFALCON TV Looping on Android Startup Screen

Customer Complaint: Television starts, shows the animated iFFALCON logo, and restarts automatically without ever reaching the Android desktop.

Defect Analysis: System partition corruption caused by sudden power disruption while Android was writing background update files.

Technician fix: Connects USB service console, enters fastboot mode, wipes damaged cache, and loads factory iFFALCON software image.
Power Circuit Fault #3

iFFALCON TV Dead with No Standby Light

Customer Complaint: The front indicator light remains unlit despite verifying the wall outlet, and the television is completely lifeless.

Defect Analysis: High voltage surge penetrated the primary filter section, destroying the safety fuse and shorting the main switching FET.

Technician fix: Replaces input fuse, checks bridge rectifier diodes, and replaces shorted MOSFET and PWM driver on the power board.
Audio Issue Fault #4

iFFALCON Internal Speakers Cracking on High Volume

Customer Complaint: Speech is barely intelligible due to constant buzzing and rattling coming from inside the television cabinet.

Defect Analysis: Speaker cone membrane has detached along the perimeter from acoustic fatigue and heat exposure.

Technician fix: Removes back chassis, verifies audio amplifier cleanliness, and installs an original matched stereo speaker module.
Display Timing Fault #5

Horizontal Ghosting Lines on iFFALCON Panel

Customer Complaint: Faint horizontal lines roll across the display, causing text and faces to appear blurred or doubled.

Defect Analysis: Clock signal timing imbalance on the display logic board or micro-oxidation on the flexible panel ribbon leads.

Technician fix: Cleans ribbon cable golden pins with deoxidizing cleaner, verifies VGH/VGL voltage lines on logic board, and inspects glass interface.
Wireless Link Fault #6

iFFALCON TV Refusing to Connect to Wi-Fi

Customer Complaint: Network menu shows Wi-Fi turned off, or scan list fails to locate the home broadband router sitting in the same room.

Defect Analysis: Degraded 3.3V power regulator feeding the wireless daughterboard or physical fracture in the internal ribbon link.

Technician fix: Inspects wireless PCB voltage rail, refits internal antenna connection, or installs an original replacement Wi-Fi module.
